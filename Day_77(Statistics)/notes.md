# Day 77 — Statistics Introduction for Data Science & AI

Today I began learning statistics, which forms the foundation of data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

---

## 📊 What is Statistics?

Statistics is the study of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data to make informed decisions.

---

## 🧠 Why Statistics Matters in AI

- Measures model accuracy  
- Explains uncertainty  
- Helps compare algorithms  
- Improves predictions  
- Prevents misleading conclusions  

---

## 🔍 Where Statistics is Used

- Data analysis  
- Machine learning models  
- AI predictions  
- Business decisions  
- Research & experiments  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Statistics turns data into knowledge and confidence into decisions.
