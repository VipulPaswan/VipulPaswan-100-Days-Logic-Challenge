# Day 78 — Measures of Central Tendency

Today I studied measures of central tendency, which describe the central point of a dataset.

---

## 📊 What is Central Tendency?

Central tendency is a statistical measure that represents the center or typical value of a dataset.

---

## 🧠 Types of Central Tendency

### Mean
- Arithmetic average  
- Sensitive to outliers  

### Median
- Middle value after sorting  
- Robust to outliers  

### Mode
- Most frequent value  
- Useful for categorical data  

---

## 🎯 When to Use

- Mean → Normal distribution  
- Median → Skewed data  
- Mode → Repeated categories  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Central tendency helps summarize data using meaningful averages.
