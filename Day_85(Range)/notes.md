# Day 85 — Range Explained with Examples

Today I studied Range, the simplest measure of dispersion in statistics.

---

## 📊 What is Range?

Range is the difference between the maximum and minimum values in a dataset.

Range = Max − Min

---

## 🔢 Example

Dataset: 10, 15, 20, 25, 40  
Min = 10  
Max = 40  

Range = 40 − 10 = 30

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Uses only two values  
- Affected by outliers  
- Ignores data distribution  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Range is useful for quick analysis but insufficient for detailed insights.
