# Day 97 — Types of Correlation

Today I studied the different types of correlation used in statistics and data science.

---

## 📊 What is Correlation?

Correlation measures how two variables move in relation to each other.

---

## 🔗 Types of Correlation

### Positive Correlation
- Both variables move in same direction

### Negative Correlation
- Variables move in opposite directions

### Zero Correlation
- No relationship between variables

---

## 🎯 Why Correlation Matters

- Helps identify patterns
- Used in feature selection
- Guides model building

---

## 🏁 Summary

Correlation describes how variables are related, not why.
