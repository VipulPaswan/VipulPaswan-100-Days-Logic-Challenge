# Day 88 — Interquartile Range (IQR) Explained

Today I studied the Interquartile Range (IQR), a robust measure of dispersion.

---

## 📊 What is IQR?

Interquartile Range measures the spread of the middle 50% of the dataset.

IQR = Q3 − Q1

---

## 🧠 Why IQR is Important

- Ignores extreme values  
- Best for skewed data  
- Used in box plots  
- Helps detect outliers  

---

## 🎯 Usage

- Statistical analysis  
- Data preprocessing  
- Machine learning pipelines  

---

## 🏁 Summary

IQR is a reliable way to measure data spread without being affected by outliers.
