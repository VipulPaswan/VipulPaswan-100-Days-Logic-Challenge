# Day 80 — Geometric Mean Explained

Today I studied the Geometric Mean, a measure of central tendency used for multiplicative data.

---

## 📊 What is Geometric Mean?

Geometric Mean is the n-th root of the product of n positive values.

---

## 🧠 When to Use Geometric Mean

- Growth rates  
- Percentage changes  
- Financial returns  
- Compounded data  

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Cannot be used with zero or negative values  
- Less intuitive than arithmetic mean  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Geometric Mean is the right average when data grows multiplicatively rather than additively.
