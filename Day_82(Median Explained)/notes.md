# Day 82 — Median Explained (Odd & Even Data Sets)

Today I studied the Median, a measure of central tendency that represents the middle value of a dataset.

---

## 📊 What is Median?

Median is the value that lies at the center of a sorted dataset.

---

## 🔢 Median Calculation

### Odd Number of Observations
- Median is the middle value after sorting.

### Even Number of Observations
- Median is the average of the two middle values.

---

## 🎯 Why Median is Important

- Not affected by outliers  
- Best for skewed data  
- Represents true center  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Median is the most reliable measure when data contains extreme values.
