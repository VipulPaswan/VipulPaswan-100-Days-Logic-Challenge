# Day 84 — Measures of Dispersion

Today I studied measures of dispersion, which describe how spread out data values are.

---

## 📊 What is Dispersion?

Dispersion measures the variability or spread of data around a central value.

---

## 🔢 Types of Dispersion

### Range
- Difference between maximum and minimum values  
- Simple but affected by outliers  

### Variance
- Average of squared deviations from mean  
- Measures overall variability  

### Standard Deviation
- Square root of variance  
- Most widely used dispersion measure  

---

## 🎯 Why Dispersion is Important

- Shows data consistency  
- Helps compare datasets  
- Used in model evaluation  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Dispersion explains how much data values differ from the average.
