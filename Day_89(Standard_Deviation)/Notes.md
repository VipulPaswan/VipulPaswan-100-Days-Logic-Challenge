# Day 89 — Standard Deviation Made Simple

Today I studied Standard Deviation, a key measure of data dispersion.

---

## 📊 What is Standard Deviation?

Standard Deviation measures how much data values deviate from the mean.

It is the square root of variance.

---

## 🧠 Interpretation

- Low SD → values are close to mean  
- High SD → values are spread out  

---

## 🎯 Why Standard Deviation Matters

- Shows data consistency  
- Used in statistics & ML  
- Helps compare datasets  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Standard Deviation tells how stable or variable the data is.
