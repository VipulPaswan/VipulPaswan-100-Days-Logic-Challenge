# Day 90 — Variance Explained with Examples

Today I studied Variance, a key measure of dispersion in statistics.

---

## 📊 What is Variance?

Variance measures how far data values are from the mean by averaging squared deviations.

---

## 🔢 Example

Dataset: 2, 4, 6  
Mean = 4  

Deviations: −2, 0, 2  
Squared deviations: 4, 0, 4  

Variance = (4 + 0 + 4) / 3 = 2.67

---

## 🎯 Why Variance is Important

- Measures data spread  
- Basis of standard deviation  
- Used in ML algorithms  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Variance explains how much data values differ from the average.
