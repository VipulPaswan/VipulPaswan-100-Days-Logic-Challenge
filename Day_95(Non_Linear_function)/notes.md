# Day 95 — Non-Linear Functions & Why AI Needs Them

Today I studied non-linear functions and their importance in AI and ML.

---

## 📐 What is a Non-Linear Function?

A non-linear function does not produce a straight-line graph and models complex relationships.

---

## 🧠 Why Linear Models Fail

- Can capture only simple patterns
- Cannot model complex real-world data

---

## 🤖 Role in AI

- Introduced via activation functions
- Enables neural networks to learn complex patterns
- Essential for deep learning

---

## 🏁 Summary

Non-linearity is the core reason AI can solve complex problems.
