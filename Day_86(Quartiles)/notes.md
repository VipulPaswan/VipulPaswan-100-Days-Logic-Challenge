# Day 86 — Quartiles Explained

Today I studied Quartiles, which divide data into four equal parts to analyze distribution.

---

## 📊 What are Quartiles?

Quartiles split a sorted dataset into four equal parts, each representing 25% of the data.

---

## 🔢 Types of Quartiles

### Q1 (First Quartile)
- Lowest 25% of the data lies below this value

### Q2 (Second Quartile)
- Median of the dataset

### Q3 (Third Quartile)
- Highest 25% of the data lies above this value

---

## 🎯 Why Quartiles are Important

- Identify data spread  
- Detect outliers  
- Understand distribution shape  

---

## 🏁 Summary

Quartiles help divide data into meaningful sections for deeper analysis.
