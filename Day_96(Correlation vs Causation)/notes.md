# Day 96 — Correlation vs Causation Explained

Today I studied the difference between correlation and causation, a critical concept in data science.

---

## 📊 Correlation

Correlation measures the relationship between two variables.

- Variables move together
- Does not imply cause-effect

---

## 📊 Causation

Causation means one variable directly influences another.

- Cause leads to effect
- Must be scientifically validated

---

## 🎯 Why This Distinction Matters

- Prevents false assumptions
- Improves model reliability
- Essential for ethical AI

---

## 🏁 Summary

Correlation shows patterns.  
Causation explains reasons.
